About Us

Sangam Pump has been a trusted manufacturer and supplier of submersible pumps and motors since 2010. Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, we focus on delivering reliable products with durable designs and strong customer service.

From residential borewells to agricultural and industrial applications, our products are built to perform in demanding conditions while maintaining energy efficiency and low maintenance requirements.
